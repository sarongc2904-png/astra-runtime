# Acceptance criteria

The POC is not considered successful because it runs. It must preserve user facts and commercial semantics.

Minimum checks for the canonical Método 360 brief:

- product remains `Método 360`;
- product type remains `minicurso grabado`;
- price remains `400 MXN`;
- buyer remains `dueñas de estéticas` in `México`;
- campaign business objective remains `vender el minicurso`;
- `consulta → conversación → cita` is treated as the teaching/conversion mechanism, not silently promoted into the campaign objective;
- invented metrics, results, CAC, ROAS, LTV, testimonials, or evidence are prohibited;
- new strategic ideas must be explicitly identified as proposals rather than facts;
- the output covers market context, ICP, offer, funnel, creative, paid media, WhatsApp conversion, and measurement when the user asks for a Campaign360 plan.
